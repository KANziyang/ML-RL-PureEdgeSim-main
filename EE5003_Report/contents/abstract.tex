\begin{abstract}

Task offloading in 6G edge--cloud systems must coordinate execution placement and wireless Physical Resource Block (PRB) reservation under mobility, heterogeneous resources, and link contention. This report studies joint task offloading and PRB allocation in a three-tier LOCAL--EDGE--CLOUD architecture, where static schedulers struggle to balance service quality and resource usage in dynamic conditions.

To address this problem, the report proposes an on-policy Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (MAPPO) scheduler formulated as a decentralized partially observable Markov decision process. Each task-generating device is modeled as an agent, and the policy is trained with centralized training and decentralized execution in a customized PureEdgeSim Java--Python co-simulation environment. A shared-policy PPO implementation serves as a controlled learning baseline under the same environment interface.

Experiments use a standard setting with 160 devices and a 30-minute simulation horizon. In the fixed-seed offline comparison, MAPPO achieves a task success rate of 99.96\%, PPO reaches 99.94\%, and the strongest heuristic baseline, ROUND\_ROBIN, reaches 90.39\%. In a separate three-seed evaluation, MAPPO attains a mean success rate of 99.9237\%, indicating stable performance in the tested setting. Ablation results show that adaptive PRB control is the dominant source of improvement: fixing PRB allocation reduces mean success to 92.3822\%, while removing agent-specific conditioning yields only a smaller reliability loss.

Overall, the results support on-policy reinforcement learning as an effective approach for joint offloading and radio-resource control in the studied simulator setting. MAPPO is best interpreted as a structurally well-matched per-device formulation, with aggregate performance comparable to PPO and a narrower reliability-oriented advantage in the present experiments.


\vspace{1em}

\textbf{Keywords:} Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, MAPPO, PPO, Task Offloading, PRB Allocation, 6G Edge-Cloud Computing, PureEdgeSim.

\end{abstract}
